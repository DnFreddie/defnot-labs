# Installing Packages is What We Live For

Look, as Linux enthusiasts, we live for two things: tinkering with configs and installing packages. There's something weirdly satisfying about getting that perfect package setup running smoothly.

But here's the thing  RHEL's basic repositories? Yeah, they're not going to cut it for most real-world scenarios.

## Why This Matters (A Lot)

In the real world, you're dealing with tons of packages from different repositories. Your systems need specific versions, custom repos, third-party software, and all kinds of package management headaches.

During the RHCE exam, they're going to test whether you actually know how to manage repositories and packages properly. Miss this, and you'll fail tasks that depend on having the right software installed.

## What We're Covering

**Step 1: Repository Management**
Set up custom repos so your packages are actually available to install.

**Step 2: Package Installation & Management**
Master the different ways to install, update, and manage packages with Ansible.
